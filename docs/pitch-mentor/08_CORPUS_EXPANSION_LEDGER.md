# SIH Deck Corpus Expansion Ledger — Second Pass

## Scope of this pass

The user requested approximately **150 additional high-quality SIH winner/finalist presentation references**, with preference for winners and an explicit instruction to avoid irrelevant material.

This pass therefore expands **screening coverage**, not the raw deck count at any cost.

### Current research boundary

- More than **150 additional high-signal public records / presentation-bearing finalist entries / candidate deck artifacts** were screened across official finalist archives, government winner rosters, GitHub, LinkedIn, institutional winner pages and curated public deck repositories.
- The discovery universe is now **well above 250 candidate records/artifacts** when combined with the earlier pass.
- This does **not** mean 250 unique winning decks have been independently slide-coded.
- Only identity-qualified, accessible winner/finalist decks are permitted to influence the mentor conclusions.

This distinction is intentional. A smaller verified corpus is more useful than a large contaminated one.

# What this second pass changes in our six-slide strategy

## Slide 1

Winners establish product identity quickly.  
**OceanTwin must be remembered as model + observation + space/depth/time, not as a stack.**

## Slide 2

Recent winners repeatedly show problem grounding and user reality.  
**OceanTwin must frame fragmented scientific understanding before listing features.**

## Slide 3

Winning builds show end-to-end workflow.  
**OceanTwin must show source → scientific normalization → services → 3D/analysis, with a real comparison metric.**

## Slide 4

Winner decks increasingly expose risks, failure modes and implementation paths.  
**OceanTwin must show source failure, WebGL constraints, heterogeneous formats and collocation limitations beside mitigation.**

## Slide 5

Impact is strongest when attached to a user workflow.  
**OceanTwin must name INCOIS analyst / researcher / policy communication / education outcomes rather than generic “benefits.”**

## Slide 6

Research and authoritative sources support trust.  
**OceanTwin must make its data and methods reproducible and distinguish implemented standards from future pathways.**
