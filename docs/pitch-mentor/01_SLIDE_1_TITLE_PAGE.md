# Slide 1 Mentor Guide — Title Page

## Official role of this slide

Slide 1 is not a decorative cover. It must establish, in seconds:

1. what problem statement this is;
2. what the solution is called;
3. what category/theme it belongs to;
4. which team is submitting it;
5. what the product does in one memorable line.

A judge should not need Slide 2 to understand the basic product category.

---

## What strong SIH decks teach us

Across winner and finalist decks, the best title pages are usually restrained. They avoid spending scarce space on biographies, long slogans, or a technology logo wall.

The recurring high-value pattern is:

**official identity → product identity → one-line value proposition → one dominant visual**

The dominant visual should preview the product, not merely decorate it.

For OceanTwin, that means a strong 3D Indian Ocean / water-column visual is more useful than generic ocean photography.

---

## Judge question to answer

> **What exactly has this team built, for whom, and why should I remember it?**

The answer must be visible before anyone speaks.

### Product identity

**OceanTwin 3D**

### Recommended one-line value proposition

> **One browser-native workspace for ocean models and real observations across space, depth and time.**

Alternative, slightly more technical:

> **A scientifically traceable 3D ocean workspace integrating model fields and in-situ observations across space, depth and time.**

Use only one.

---

## Recommended visual hierarchy

### Top strip

Official SIH identity and PS metadata. Keep this compact.

### Center-left

Product name:
**OceanTwin 3D**

One-line value proposition immediately below.

### Center-right / dominant background

A clean visual combining:

- Indian Ocean geographic context;
- real ocean-field coloration;
- clear depth cue / water-column transition;
- one or two observation markers.

Do not overcrowd the cover with charts.

### Bottom proof strip

Three small evidence anchors are enough:

**3D WATER COLUMN**  
**REAL OBSERVATIONS**  
**SPACE · DEPTH · TIME**

These are not marketing badges. They communicate the three product pillars.

---

## What not to put on Slide 1

Avoid:

- full architecture;
- ten technology logos;
- team-member photographs;
- paragraphs;
- “AI-powered” unless an actual AI component is central and defensible;
- “real-time” unless the exact data path is truly real-time;
- “global digital twin” if the demonstrated evidence is a bounded Indian Ocean window;
- unsupported claims such as “world's first,” “100% accurate,” or “production-ready.”

---

## Why this is the right learning from winners

The strongest decks reduce the judge's cognitive load immediately. The title page establishes a product category and then lets later slides prove the claim.

For OceanTwin, the cover should make the judge think:

> “This is a serious 3D ocean-analysis product that combines models and measurements.”

Not:

> “This team used Cesium, FastAPI and some ocean data.”

The implementation stack is evidence, not the identity.

---

## Speaker intent

The opening sentence should be short enough to say while the judge is still looking at the cover.

Recommended intent:

> “OceanTwin 3D brings numerical ocean fields and real observations into one browser-native workspace, so the Indian Ocean can be explored across latitude, longitude, depth and time instead of through disconnected 2D tools.”

Do not start by listing the team or technology stack.

---

## Acceptance criteria

Slide 1 passes only if:

- official PS identity is exact;
- a first-time viewer can describe the product after 10–15 seconds;
- one dominant visual communicates ocean + 3D/depth;
- the value proposition is one sentence;
- there are no unsupported operational or AI claims;
- the slide is visually lighter than the technical slides;
- no required official template field is accidentally removed.

---

## Fail-safe rule

If the final product screenshot is visually weak or uncertain:

1. use a verified clean OceanTwin explorer render;
2. do not fabricate an ocean rendering;
3. do not use a generic stock-ocean hero that misrepresents the product;
4. preserve the official template identity first;
5. simplify rather than add decoration.

---

## Final mentor test

Hide every logo and team field for five seconds.

If the remaining slide still says:

**OceanTwin 3D → model + observation → space + depth + time**

the slide is doing its job.
